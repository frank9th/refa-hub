Phase 1: Registration
Complete registration, pay fee, upload passport photo and performance video (if required), accept
terms, download confirmation.
Phase 2: Audition
Attend on time, present ID, complete accreditation, perform, attend interview if required.

Check results, confirm participation, attend orientation, sign agreement, submit documents.
Phase 4: Training & Mentorship
Attend rehearsals, coaching, media sessions, follow schedule, work with mentors.
Phase 5: Competition
Prepare performances, costume fitting, technical rehearsals, arrive before call time, perform, follow
backstage instructions.
Phase 6: Promotion
Share voting link, promote on social media, attend interviews and publicity events.
Phase 7: Voting
Encourage official voting, avoid fraud, report suspicious activity.
Phase 8: Grand Finale
Attend rehearsals, interviews, perform, awards ceremony, official photos.
