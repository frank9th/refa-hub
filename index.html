Community Engine

Parent Participation Strategy

Parents are not spectators — they are campaign managers, content contributors, and the primary vote mobilisers. Integrate them from Day 1.

💡A motivated parent with 200 WhatsApp contacts, converting at 10%, generates 20 votes x ₦200 = ₦4,000 per parent. With 100 parents: ₦400,000 from parents alone.

WhatsApp Groups Per Team

10 groups, mentor as admin, all parents added Audition Day. Voting links, schedules, content to share, team morale.

"Families Behind the Word" Series

30-sec parent video: "My child is competing because..." Family devotion photo + quote. Published daily Profile Week. Highest organic reach.

Reserved Family Corner at Events

Dedicated seating with team-colour banners. Parent Supporter Card on arrival. Photo station at branded backdrop. Stage introduction by team name.

Voting Starter Pack

Printed QR code card + voting link. WhatsApp message templates. Challenge card: "Can your family get 100 votes before Stage 1?" Top-voting parent gets a stage shoutout.

Parent Awards at Grand Final

Most Supportive Family. Faithful Presence Award (all 3 stages attended). Community Champion Award. Nominated before the Final to drive attendance.

Parent Testimony Moment

2-minute segment before Grand Final results. 2-3 parents, 30 seconds each. Prompt: "What has REFA done for my child?" Emotional peak of the evening.

🔌 REFA Zero-Data Local Hub — 4 Connection Methods

Connect your team's smartphones, cameras, and laptops to the Media Lead's hub directory over local Wi-Fi without internet or expensive mobile data!

🌐 Method 1: Instant Browser Web Drop (Any Phone or PC)

RECOMMENDED FOR QUICK UPLOADS

No app installation required. Any team member can open their browser and upload 4K video or photo dumps directly.

  1. Connect phone or laptop to the Media Lead's Wi-Fi Hotspot or Venue Wi-Fi router.
  2. Go to one of the dynamic endpoints detected from this host machine:
    📡 Detecting host machine network endpoints...
  3. Go to Media Gallery or Media Vault $\rightarrow$ Use the zero-data transfer form or direct download buttons to preview and transfer files.
⚡ Live upload progress bar shows 0% to 100% completion in real-time.

📁 Method 2: Direct Network Shared Directory (Save Directly to Folder)

DIRECT FOLDER MOUNT

Mount the hub directory as a network drive on team devices so videographers can copy or save heavy files directly into the host folder.

🪟 Windows File Explorer

Press Win + R and type:

\\<HOST-IP>\uploads
Drag and drop heavy 4K camera files directly!
🍎 Mac Finder

In Finder, press Cmd + K and type:

smb://<HOST-IP>/uploads
Mounted shared folder appears on your Mac desktop.
📱 Mobile (Android / iOS)

Open CX File Explorer or iOS Files app:

Add Network → SMB → <HOST-IP>
Copy photos directly from phone gallery into hub!
