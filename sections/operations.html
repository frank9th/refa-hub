Key Operational Systems & Staff Roles

Clear role definitions, headcount requirements, deployment stations, and duty directives to run a seamless, professional production.

🧭 Production Principle: Every team member has a single primary deployment zone during live events. No cross-station confusion.
👥 Operational Roles & Staff Deployment Directives
Role Base & Title Staff Count Station / Location Duty Directives & Key Responsibilities
Stage Manager 1 Lead Stage Wings & Backstage Directs contestant stage entry/exit; queues Emcee running order; oversees stage props, podium timers, and live judge score sheet handoffs.
Social Media Managers 2 Persons Media Desk & Mobile Audience Executes real-time Instagram/TikTok stories, live updates every 30 mins, audience reaction clips, hashtag monitoring, and instant result posts.
Videographers / Photographers 2 Persons Center Aisle & Stage Front Operates multi-camera HD video recording of recitations, judge remarks, contestant facial expressions, red carpet entrance, and crowd reactions.
Registration & Check-in Leads 2 Persons Auditorium Lobby Entrance Manages contestant registration, issues name badges & team tags, distributes Parent Voting Starter Packs, and verifies parent WhatsApp group joins.
Voting System Coordinator 1 Lead Control Booth / Ops Desk Monitors 3rd-party online voting portal dashboard in real-time, runs anti-fraud checks, tallies cumulative scores, and hands sealed leaderboard to Emcee.
Sponsorship & Guest Liaison 1 Lead VIP Seating & Entrance Receives VIP guests, partner pastors, and corporate sponsors; escorts them to reserved VIP tables; ensures sponsor banners are properly mounted.
Contestant Welfare & Chaperones 3 Persons Contestant Holding Room Manages 100 contestants in holding hall; organizes team lineup 2 rounds before appearance; provides water, encouragement, and quiet preparation space.
MC / Host & Co-Host 2 Persons Main Stage Podium Drives event momentum, introduces contestants & mentors, delivers sponsor acknowledgments, reads live voting leaderboards, and keeps time.
Sound & Lighting Technicians 2 Engineers A/V Control Console Manages podium microphones, ambient lighting, countdown timer display on main screens, audio playback for team entrances, and live stream feed.
📍 Event Day Deployment Map

1. Auditorium Lobby (Entrance)

Registration Desk (2 Leads) · Photo Backdrop Station · Parent Supporter Card Distribution.

2. Main Auditorium Sanctuary

VIP Sponsor Tables (1 Liaison) · Family Seating Corners (Team Banners) · Stage Front Photographers (2 Camera Ops).

3. Stage & Wings

Podium MCs (2 Hosts) · Judges Table (3 Judges) · Stage Manager (1 Wing Lead).

4. Control Booth & Holding Room

Voting & Live Stream Console (1 Coordinator + A/V Techs) · Contestant Holding Room (3 Chaperones).

📲 Communication Systems & Channels

Team Mentor WhatsApp Groups

10 separate groups (1 per team). Mentor is admin. Used for weekly coaching schedules, voting link broadcasts, and parent updates.

REFA Master Operations Broadcast

Broadcast list containing all 100 contestants and parents for urgent official announcements and stage arrival times.

Shared Media Cloud (Drive)

Centralized Google Drive folder for Videographers, Photographers, and Social Media Leads to drop raw footage for instant editing.
